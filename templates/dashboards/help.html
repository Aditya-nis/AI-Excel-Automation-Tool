📖 End-to-End Operational Pipeline

The AI Power BI platform follows an immutable, governed business intelligence lifecycle:

1. Ingestion
Upload CSV/XLSX. Files are validated, fingerprinted with SHA-256, and stored as immutable Parquet versions.
2. Profiling
Automated statistical profiling calculates quartiles, null percentages, distinct cardinality, and IQR outliers.
3. Cleaning & Undo
Reversible transformation engine saves atomic derived versions (v2, v3). 1-click instant rollback to any parent.
4. Reconciliation
Financial control totals verify that inputs match outputs. Critical breaks trigger publication blocks.
5. MIS Publication
Daily, Weekly, Monthly, and Yearly reports are compiled into executive PDFs, formatted Excel, and raw CSV.
📐 Formula & Integrity Standards
  • Zero Fabrication Rule: No fake KPIs or hallucinations. If data is absent, honest empty states are rendered.
  • Decimal Precision: Financial numbers use Python `Decimal` arithmetic with round half-up behavior.
  • Safe Division: Division by zero evaluates to None with documented notice, never infinite or fabricated numbers.
  • Growth Rates: (Current - Baseline) / |Baseline| * 100. When baseline is 0, growth returns explicit indeterminate notice.
